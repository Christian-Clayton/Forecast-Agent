# Literature — Critical Thinking and Argument Evaluation

Reference list only — not loaded by default. ~20 sources synthesized into the SKILL.md's core principles.

## Argumentation Theory

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *The Uses of Argument* | Stephen Toulmin | Origin of the Toulmin model (claim, grounds, warrant, backing, rebuttal) |
| *A Rulebook for Arguments* | Anthony Weston | Concise, practical guide to constructing and evaluating arguments |
| *Attacking Faulty Reasoning* | T. Edward Damer | Comprehensive fallacy taxonomy with a systematic classification scheme |
| *Being Logical* | D.Q. McInerny | Accessible introduction to formal and informal logic |
| *Thank You for Arguing* | Jay Heinrichs | Practical rhetoric — persuasion techniques, useful both for arguing well and recognizing them used on you |

## Falsifiability and Scientific Reasoning

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* | Karl Popper | Falsifiability as the demarcation criterion — a claim that can't be proven wrong isn't scientifically meaningful |
| *The Demon-Haunted World* | Carl Sagan | The "baloney detection kit" — a practical checklist for evaluating extraordinary claims |
| *Intuition Pumps and Other Tools for Thinking* | Daniel Dennett | Includes Dennett's rules for constructive criticism — the source of the modern "steelman" practice |

## Evidence Quality and Statistical Skepticism

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Calling Bullshit: The Art of Skepticism in a Data-Driven World* | Carl Bergstrom & Jevin West | Modern, data-literacy-focused treatment of evaluating quantitative claims |
| *How to Lie with Statistics* | Darrell Huff | The classic short treatment of statistical manipulation tactics |
| *Bad Science* | Ben Goldacre | Applied media/pharma-industry case studies of misused evidence |
| GRADE framework (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluations) | GRADE Working Group | The medical field's standard for grading evidence quality/strength of recommendation |
| Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews | Cochrane Collaboration | Reference methodology for the evidence-quality hierarchy used in the SKILL.md's tiering table |

## Persuasion Tactics (Recognizing Them Used On You)

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion* | Robert Cialdini | Catalog of persuasion principles (reciprocity, social proof, authority, scarcity) — useful for recognizing when they're being deployed on you rather than genuine evidence |
| *Merchants of Doubt* | Naomi Oreskes & Erik Conway | Case studies of manufactured scientific doubt as a rhetorical/PR tactic |

## Mindset and Practice

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *The Scout Mindset* | Julia Galef | "Scout" (accuracy-seeking) vs. "soldier" (defend-your-side) mindset — directly complements steelmanning |
| *Factfulness* | Hans Rosling | Common systematic misconceptions in interpreting world data, with a practical checklist |
| *How to Read a Book* | Mortimer Adler & Charles Van Doren | Analytical reading — how to actually extract and evaluate an author's argument rather than skim it |
