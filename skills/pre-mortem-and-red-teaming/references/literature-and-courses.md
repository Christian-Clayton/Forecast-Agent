# Literature — Pre-Mortem and Red-Teaming

Reference list only — not loaded by default. Sources synthesized into the SKILL.md's core principles.

## Origin and Empirical Basis

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| "Performing a Project Premortem," Harvard Business Review, 2007 | Gary Klein | Popularized and named the business "premortem" technique used directly in this skill |
| "Back to the Future: Temporal Perspective in the Explanation of Events," Journal of Behavioral Decision Making, 1989 | Deborah J. Mitchell, J. Edward Russo, Nancy Pennington | The empirical "prospective hindsight" finding — imagining an outcome as already having occurred increases correct-cause identification by ~30% |
| *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions* | Gary Klein | Broader naturalistic decision-making research underlying Klein's later premortem work |

## Red-Teaming and Adversarial Review

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Psychology of Intelligence Analysis*, CIA Center for the Study of Intelligence, 1999 | Richards J. Heuer Jr. | Structured analytic techniques, including devil's advocacy and red teaming, in intelligence analysis |
| *Red Team: How to Succeed by Thinking Like the Enemy*, 2015 | Micah Zenko | Survey of red-teaming practice across military, security, and business contexts |
| Red Team Handbook | US Army (University of Foreign Military and Cultural Studies) | Formal military doctrine on red-teaming methodology |
| Historical "Advocatus Diaboli" (Devil's Advocate) role in Catholic canonization process | — | One of the oldest formalized adversarial-review practices, direct precursor to modern red-teaming |

## Why It Works (Groupthink and Decision Quality)

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Victims of Groupthink*, 1972 | Irving Janis | Names the group-conformity phenomenon that premortems/red-teaming are specifically designed to counteract |
| *Thinking in Bets*, 2018 | Annie Duke | Popular treatment connecting premortems and adversarial review to decision quality under uncertainty |
