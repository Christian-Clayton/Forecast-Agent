# Literature & Courses — Sequential Thinking

## Foundational Books & Papers

- **Daniel Kahneman — *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011)** — System 1 vs. System 2 thinking; slow, deliberate reasoning for hard problems.
- **Herbert Simon & Allen Newell — *Human Problem Solving* (1972)** — Problem spaces, means-ends analysis, and stepwise search.
- **Charles Sanders Peirce — abductive, deductive, and inductive reasoning** — Iterative inference cycles in inquiry.
- **Nassim Nicholas Taleb — *The Black Swan* (2007)** — The limits of reasoning and the importance of revisability under uncertainty.
- **John Dewey — *How We Think* (1910)** — Reflective thought as a sequence of problem → suggestion → reasoning → testing.
