# Literature — Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

Reference list only — not loaded by default. ~20 sources synthesized into the SKILL.md's core principles. Availability of specific free/legal sources may change; verify before relying on a link.

## Foundational Decision Theory

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Theory of Games and Economic Behavior* | John von Neumann & Oskar Morgenstern | Expected utility theorem — the mathematical foundation for rational choice under uncertainty |
| *The Foundations of Decision Analysis* (and related papers) | Ronald A. Howard | Founded the field of decision analysis; formalized decision trees and value-of-information |
| *Decisions with Multiple Objectives* | Ralph Keeney & Howard Raiffa | Multi-criteria decision analysis when objectives can't be reduced to one number |
| *Models of Man* / theory of bounded rationality | Herbert Simon | "Satisficing" — real decision-makers optimize against limited time/information, not true global optima |
| Prospect Theory papers (1979) | Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky | Empirically, people weight losses more than equivalent gains and distort probabilities near 0/1 — the behavioral correction to pure expected-utility theory |
| Regret theory papers | Graham Loomes & Robert Sugden | Formal minimax-regret alternative to expected-value maximization |

## Practical / Applied Frameworks

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Smart Choices* | John Hammond, Ralph Keeney, Howard Raiffa | The most practical, non-technical walkthrough of applied decision analysis |
| *Decisive: How to Make Better Choices in Life and Work* | Chip Heath & Dan Heath | The WRAP process (Widen, Reality-test, Attain distance, Prepare to be wrong) |
| *Real Options: Managing Strategic Investment in an Uncertain World* | Martha Amram & Nalin Kulatilaka | Pricing the value of waiting/flexibility as a financial option |
| Amazon 1997 shareholder letter (origin of "one-way door / two-way door") | Jeff Bezos | The reversibility gate — matches decision-making effort to how costly a wrong call is to undo |
| *Algorithms to Live By* | Brian Christian & Tom Griffiths | Computer-science framings (optimal stopping, explore/exploit) applied to everyday decisions |

## Behavioral Economics (Why Humans Deviate From the Theory)

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Thinking, Fast and Slow* | Daniel Kahneman | System 1/System 2, anchoring, loss aversion, framing effects |
| *Predictably Irrational* | Dan Ariely | Catalog of specific, reproducible irrational decision patterns |
| *Nudge* | Richard Thaler & Cass Sunstein | Choice architecture — how the framing of options changes the decision without changing the payoffs |
| *Misbehaving: The Making of Behavioral Economics* | Richard Thaler | History of how behavioral economics challenged pure rational-choice theory |
| *The Undoing Project* | Michael Lewis | Narrative account of the Kahneman/Tversky collaboration that produced prospect theory |

## Risk and Probability History

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
