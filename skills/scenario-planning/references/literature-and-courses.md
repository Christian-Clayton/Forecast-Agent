# Literature — Scenario Planning

Reference list only — not loaded by default. Sources synthesized into the SKILL.md's core principles.

## Origin

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *Thinking About the Unthinkable*, 1962 | Herman Kahn | RAND Corporation origin of formal scenario methodology, developed for Cold War nuclear strategy |
| "Scenarios: Uncharted Waters Ahead" and "Scenarios: Shooting the Rapids," Harvard Business Review, 1985 | Pierre Wack | The canonical Shell scenario-planning case, including the famous pre-1973 oil-shock scenario that gave Shell a head start its competitors lacked |

## Practitioner Texts ("Intuitive Logics" School)

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| *The Art of the Long View*, 1991 | Peter Schwartz | Most widely read practitioner text, from the Global Business Network (GBN) school |
| *Scenarios: The Art of Strategic Conversation*, 1996 | Kees van der Heijden | The intuitive-logics school's most rigorous academic treatment |
| *Solving Tough Problems*, 2004 | Adam Kahane | Scenario planning applied to social/political conflict — the Mont Fleur scenarios in South Africa's transition |

## Academic Grounding

| Work | Author | Core contribution |
|---|---|---|
| "Multiple Scenario Development: Its Conceptual and Behavioral Foundation," Strategic Management Journal, 1993 | Paul J. H. Schoemaker | Empirical/academic grounding for why multiple scenarios outperform single forecasts under deep uncertainty |
